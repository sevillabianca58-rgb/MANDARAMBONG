A little about me

Hi! I'm Bianca, but you can call me Sevsev. I'm a second-year Computer Science student who is still learning, exploring, and figuring things out along the way.

I'm a simple person with big dreams. One of my biggest goals is to finish my studies so I can help my family and give back to them someday. I also hope to travel to different countries, experience new cultures, and see more of the world.
