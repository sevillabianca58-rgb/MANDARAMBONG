More Than Just Code

I'm Shanne Kian A. Ramirez, a second-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student at Norzagaray College. My journey in technology is driven by curiosity, creativity, and a strong desire to create a better future for myself and the people I love.

My greatest inspiration is my mother and my family. Their sacrifices, love, and support remind me why I must keep moving forward, even when things get difficult. I carry a personal dream of overcoming the challenges of poverty, giving my mother a better life, and someday giving back to her for everything she has done for me.

Every line of code I write, every skill I learn, and every project I complete is another step toward that dream. I know I still have a lot to learn, but I believe that discipline, perseverance, and a willingness to improve can turn small beginnings into meaningful achievements.

Beyond programming, I enjoy playing online games, basketball, chess, and reading light novels, manga, manhwa, and manhua. These interests reflect my love for strategy, creativity, storytelling, and continuous discovery.

I'm not just building projects. I'm building my skills, my opportunities, and the future I want to give back to my family.
